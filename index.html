    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Is QR Lab really free to use?

    Yes, 100% free. There are no hidden fees, subscriptions, sign-ups or scan limits. You can generate as many QR codes as you want, and they will never expire.

    Do these QR codes expire?

    No. QR codes made with QR Lab are static: the content is encoded directly into the image. They keep working forever, as long as the destination (like your website link) stays online.

    How do I make QR codes in bulk?

    Open the Bulk CSV tab. Create a spreadsheet in Excel or Google Sheets with the file name in column A and the link or text in column B, save it as .csv, and upload it. QR Lab generates every code with your chosen design and downloads them together as a single ZIP file.

    Is my data private?

    Yes. Every QR code is generated locally in your browser. Your links, Wi-Fi passwords, contact details and logos are never uploaded to any server.

    Why should I add a custom logo?

    A logo in the center of your QR code increases brand recognition and trust, and people are more likely to scan a code when they recognize the brand. QR Lab automatically switches error correction to High when you add a logo so the code still scans reliably.

    What is the error correction level?

    Error correction lets a QR code be read even when part of it is covered, damaged or blurred. If you add a logo, keep error correction on High (30%) so the code scans every time.

    Can I use these QR codes for commercial print?

    Absolutely. Choose the High-Res (800px) size for a sharp PNG, or download the SVG vector file, which scales to any size without losing quality. Both are ideal for business cards, flyers, posters and packaging.

    Can I install QR Lab as an app?

    Yes. QR Lab is a Progressive Web App. Use the Install button, or Add to Home Screen on your phone, and it opens like a native app and keeps working offline.
